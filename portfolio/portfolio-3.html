Women Employed

Women Employed is a Chicago-based nonprofit dedicated to advancing women’s economic equity, removing barriers to opportunity, and advocating for fair and inclusive workplace practices. Their work supports systemic change by helping women access greater economic security and creating more equitable conditions in the workplace.

As a nonprofit organization, Women Employed had limited resources available for a new digital experience. To help support their mission, 8th Light partnered with the organization to design and build a new website on a pro bono basis. I had the opportunity to contribute to the project by helping create a digital platform that could communicate the organization’s impact, connect people with its resources, and support its broader advocacy efforts.

The website redesign took place alongside a broader organizational rebrand, requiring the digital experience to evolve in step with a new visual identity. Because Women Employed did not have a dedicated IT team, the project needed to account for more than the public-facing experience. The website’s backend also had to be intuitive, approachable, and easy to maintain for staff with limited technical or web-design experience.

We treated the organization’s internal content editors as a core user group and prioritized a content management experience that would support them long after the initial launch. We built the site on WordPress, creating a flexible and user-friendly platform that allowed staff to update content, publish new resources, and maintain the site without relying on developers or specialized technical support.

By pairing the new brand with an accessible, sustainable content-management workflow, we delivered a website that was designed not only to serve Women Employed’s audience but also to empower its team to manage and evolve the platform independently.

The team successfully delivered a bold, distinctive website on schedule and launched it ahead of Women Employed’s 50th anniversary. A major milestone that culminated in a public gala where the organization unveiled its new brand identity.

The project was an opportunity to create more than a visually engaging website. By pairing a strong new digital presence with an approachable, sustainable content-management experience, we gave the organization the tools to continue communicating its mission and supporting its work long after launch.

Contributing to this project was especially meaningful because of the organization behind it: a dedicated team advancing women’s economic equity, expanding opportunity, and advocating for fairer workplaces.
