I've done a few long bike trips before, but the holy grail trip for me was to ride the famous Great Allegheny Passage and Chesapeake & Ohio trails from Pittsburg, PA down to Washington DC. Two continuous bike paths spanning 335 miles of beautiful scenery and peaceful riding. The challenge would be riding 50-60 miles a day fully loaded for a week to 10 days. I had to figure out where I would sleep, what I would eat, and what would happen in an emergency. Most of my longer rides were within driving distance of home, so I could be rescued, but since I would be venturing out to the east coast it would be necessary to cover all my bases.

Prep

The route is 100% complete start to finish and from what I read fairly well marked so I wasn't worried baout navigation. Overnights and food were going to be the challenge. I fired up cycle.travel, simply typed in Pittsburgh to start and DC to finish. By default it puts you on paved roads the whole way, but if you select "gravel" it navigated along both paths with almost no exceptions. Cycle.travel is great because it marks off every 10 miles, so you can plan your days and camping spots accordingly. I estimated I could do about 60 miles a day comfortably fully loaded, so I started by marking off every 60 miles. Then I switched to Google Maps on each 60 mile spot and figured out where I could camp or stay overnight close by so I could maintain my pace.
